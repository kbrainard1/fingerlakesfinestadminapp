ADDYSON'S DREAM, 2021, 16.3h, bay gelding

If you prefer your horses built like a “moose”, but far, far more attractive than one, Addyson's Dream is the horse for you! This large, thick guy is a trainer favorite that they just couldn't say enough good things about.  While Addyson is vice free, he can be a bit grumpy with a hard curry.  His connections say that this big guy has a big soft spot for women and children but truly is a “people horse”.  

Addyson is reported to be very good to ride with no previous injuries.  He stood for our photo shoot like a champ, however, would have been far happier crawling into his handler's lap.  He remained respectful of his recently injured handler who had to jog a bit slow himself.  He demonstrated his naturally lofty jog and walk with over track, even with a missing hind shoe.

Addyson is by Uptowncharlybrown, who has Grand Slam and Langfuhr lines, which would suggest he will be very amateur friendly and also athletic.  He is out of a Lemon Drop Kid mare who's offspring are also known for their trainability.  We can see this very large guy excelling in just about any discipline, and with his remarkably good looks, should kill it in the show ring!  Don't let Addyson's Dream pass you by!

Please note that references will be required and checked prior to being able to purchase this horse.  Please see our post for more information.
