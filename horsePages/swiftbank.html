SWIFT BANK, 2022, 16.2h, dark bay gelding

Located at the Finger Lakes Race Track in Farmington, NY (near Rochester, NY).

Calling all ‘Swifties’! You’ll have a lot of fun coming up with a barn name for this refined, tall, dark, and very handsome boy. Plus, he’ll already have an extensive playlist! Despite his name, Swift Bank has not been as swift on the track as his connections had originally hoped for; they’ve run him a few times with generally mediocre results, so they tell us they’d prefer this young, sound, gorgeous boy to ‘shake it off’ and find a new job. So, who is ready to write a ‘love story’ with a tall, refined, lightly raced (14 starts), friendly, super-smart, and well-mannered young gelding? Here he is!

We’re told that he is typically a good boy with no stall vices or issues; he’s smart and good about everything- whether being ponied, using the Eurociser, in his stall, and he also trailers well. We met him as he was being prepped for a Monday race, he was a tad distracted but polite and easily persuaded to strike some lovely poses once we offered him some peppermints. And he was a friendly cuddlebug when we hung out chatting in front of his stall afterwards.

Did we mention that he is gorgeous? He has an intelligent and pretty face. His long, elegant neck ties into a nicely sloped shoulder. He displayed a confident walk and a springy jog with push from behind. He gave the impression of being nicely forward, polite, balanced, and clever. Bred and trained for the longer distances, we could see him having great eventing potential, but he’s athletic (and certainly pretty) enough to go in any direction that you’d like to take him.

Swift Bank is by the popular Central Banker out of an Anabaa mare with Danzig and Seattle Slew also appearing on his dam side.

An update: as an added bonus, he capped off his race career with a win on 7/27! So, now is the time to say, ‘you belong with me’ and bring him home to be your next show pony!

Please note that references will be required and checked prior to being able to purchase this horse. Please see our post pinned to the top of our FB page for more information.
