THREE CAPTAINS, 2020, 16.2 h., dark bay gelding

This stunningly photogenic boy has the classic ‘Central Banker face’ and with his well-balanced proportions, you don’t realize how tall he actually is until you stand next to him. We’re told that he’s good in his stall and to work around; his only vice is that, at the track, he can be mouthy with anyone passing by his stall. But we observed a friendly and affectionate horse who enjoyed nuzzling his handler and the volunteers. During his photoshoot, he remained exceptionally chill and polite. We liked the depth of his heart girth and admired his topline. He’s a big, gorgeous, chill horse!

He raced well in June but during a recent workout, he came back from breezing with a sore knee. No further diagnostics have been done yet, so his connections are offering at a price reflective of this. Interested parties will be carefully scrutinized to ensure that they are qualified, understand the potential commitment, and are willing / able to do the proper assessment, can evaluate the options, and conduct any appropriate rehab that is required. Note: excellent vet references will be required and carefully checked!

For his video, he walked quietly and politely on a loose rein displaying a relaxed, swinging stride with a nice overstep. We do not have a jog video from our visit to post as we knew that he was sore on his knee. However, we did find a jog snippet from his last race on 06/10/26; we drooled over his light, fluid, and floaty movement. This lovely gelding is a good-looking, big, young, nice moving, and super chill horse and we feel that he is definitely be worth the investment to rehab! That’s if one of our volunteers don’t scoop him up first!

Three Captains is by Central Banker out of a Belong to Me mare with Danzig and Dynaformer also appearing on his distaff side. Located at the Finger Lakes Race Track in Farmington, NY (near Rochester, NY).

Please note that references will be required and checked prior to being able to purchase this horse.  Please see our post for more information.

Price: $1,000 to qualified party, due to special situation
