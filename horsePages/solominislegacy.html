SOLOMINI’S LEGACY, 2022, 16.1h chestnut gelding

PLACED

Located and Finger Lakes Racetrack in Farmington, NY (near Rochester)

We met a true sweetheart today when this handsome chestnut with the kindest eyes came out of his stall sporting his copper coat and flaxen mane. He walked out on a loose leadline and remained relaxed throughout our visit. He was very happy to visit with us and seemed to enjoy all the attention while remaining as lovable as a golden retriever puppy. (Maybe that’s where the flaxen mane comes from?) He stood for kisses, treats and posing and jogged quietly. He seemed to be an all-around “chill” guy. At just 4 years old it turns out this very sweet guy is looking for his next career because he is simply “as slow as molasses”.

He is by Solomini, son of much desired Curlin, known for their trainability and athleticism, and is out of Bella’s Flare by Bellamy Road, known for offspring with incredible movement and brains. His handler says he is easy to work around in the stall where he is also neat and clean. He has no vices and turns out well in a group. Best of all, he has no “known injuries” with clean legs with lovely feet. His video says it all.

Please note that references will be required and checked prior to being able to purchase this horse. Please see our post for more information.

Offered at $3,500.
